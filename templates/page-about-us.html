About Us

Maison Merch is created by a group of Black founders who have lived the global fan experience. From Lagos to Toronto. From New York to Accra. From London to Houston.

Who We Are

We've Been You.

We've been the fans in the stadium. The people standing in merch lines. The immigrants cheering loudly with our flags. The families shopping for affordable fan gear. The diaspora that turns any match into a cultural festival.

We know what it's like to want to represent your country, your culture, your identity — and find that the options are either too expensive, too generic, or just not made with you in mind.

So we built something better.

"
We've seen families forced to walk away from stadium merch. Kids crying because they couldn't get a flag. Diaspora fans creating their own makeshift gear. Watch parties missing the vibe because decorations were too expensive or too scattered.
— The Maison Merch Founders
Our "Aha" Moment

Representation shouldn't be a luxury. Fans shouldn't have to choose between joy and affordability.

We are not in the "merch" business. We are in the cultural experience business.
