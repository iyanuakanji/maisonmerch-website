Your satisfaction is important to us. If you're not happy with your order, we want to make it right. Please read our refund policy carefully before submitting a request.

Refund Eligibility

To be eligible for a refund, your request must meet the following conditions:

  • The refund request must be submitted within 30 days of the delivery date
  • The item must be in its original, unused condition with original packaging and all included accessories
  • You must have a valid proof of purchase (order confirmation email or order number)
  • Items must not have been used, washed, altered, or damaged by the customer

Refund Timeline

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we will process your refund within 5–7 business days. Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout.

Please allow an additional 3–5 business days for your bank or credit card provider to process and reflect the refund in your account balance.

Heads up: Processing times may vary depending on your bank. If you haven't received your refund after 10 business days from our confirmation email, please contact your financial institution before reaching out to us.

Damaged, Defective, or Wrong Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, please contact us within 7 days of delivery at info@maisonmerch.ca with:

  • Your order number
  • A description of the issue
  • Clear photos of the item and packaging

In these cases, we will cover all return shipping costs and issue a full refund or replacement at no additional cost to you.

Non-Refundable Items & Situations

The following are not eligible for a refund:

  • Items returned after the 30-day refund window
  • Items that have been used, washed, or damaged after delivery
  • Items returned without original packaging or missing components
  • Change-of-mind returns for sale items purchased at greater than 50% discount (unless defective)
  • Digital products or gift cards
  • Shipping fees (original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error)

How to Request a Refund

  1. Email info@maisonmerch.ca with the subject line "Refund Request — [Your Order Number]"
  2. Include a brief explanation and any supporting photos
  3. Our team will respond within 2 business days with instructions
  4. If approved, ship the item back using a trackable service and share the tracking number with us
  5. Once received, your refund will be processed within 5–7 business days
