Refunds via Amazon

Because all Maison Merch products are sold and fulfilled through Amazon, all refund requests are handled directly by Amazon under their standard buyer protection policy. This means:

  • Most items are eligible for a refund within 30 days of delivery
  • Refunds are returned to your original Amazon payment method
  • Amazon typically processes refunds within 3–5 business days of receiving the return
  • If your item arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect, Amazon will cover return shipping and issue a full refund or replacement
Amazon handles it. You don't need to contact Maison Merch to initiate a refund — go directly to your Amazon account for the fastest resolution. Amazon's team is available 24/7.

How to Request a Refund

  1. Log into your Amazon account
  2. Go to Returns & Orders
  3. Find your Maison Merch order and select Return or Replace Items
  4. Select your reason and follow the instructions
  5. Once Amazon receives the return, your refund is processed automatically

For full details, see Amazon's Return & Refund Policy.
