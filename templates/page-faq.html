Orders & Products

How do I place an order?

Ordering is easy! Browse our fan bundles on the Shop page, select your desired bundle, and add it to your cart. Follow the checkout process to enter your shipping details and complete your payment. You'll receive an order confirmation email right away.

Can I order from outside Canada and the USA?

Currently, we ship exclusively to Canada and the United States. We're working on expanding to more regions. Stay tuned by subscribing to our newsletter for updates on international shipping.

Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?

We process orders quickly! If you need to make changes, please contact us at info@maisonmerch.ca or call +1 (343) 500-2866 within 2 hours of placing your order. After that, we cannot guarantee changes can be made before the order ships.

Will I get an order confirmation?

Yes! You'll receive an automated order confirmation email immediately after checkout. Once your order ships, you'll receive a separate shipping confirmation email with your tracking number.

Do you offer bulk or wholesale pricing?

Yes — we love supporting event organizers, schools, clubs, and diaspora associations. Reach out to us at info@maisonmerch.ca with your event details and required quantities and we'll put together a custom quote.

Shipping & Delivery

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping within Canada typically takes 5–10 business days. Shipping to the United States takes 7–14 business days. Processing time is 1–3 business days before your order ships.

Do you offer free shipping?

Yes! We offer free standard shipping on all orders over $75 CAD within Canada and the USA. Orders under $75 CAD are subject to a flat shipping rate, which is calculated at checkout.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking number. You can use this number on the carrier's website to track your package in real time.

What if my package is lost or damaged in transit?

If your package is lost or arrives damaged, please contact us immediately at info@maisonmerch.ca with your order number and photos of any damage. We'll work with the carrier to resolve the issue and make it right.

Returns & Refunds

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for items in their original, unused condition with original packaging. To initiate a return, email us at info@maisonmerch.ca with your order number and reason for return.

How do I get a refund?

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we'll process your refund within 5–7 business days. Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Please allow an additional 3–5 business days for your bank to process the refund.

Who pays for return shipping?

For returns due to a defect or our error, we'll cover the return shipping cost. For change-of-mind returns, the customer is responsible for return shipping fees. We recommend using a trackable shipping method to ensure your return arrives safely.

Can I exchange an item?

We currently process exchanges on a case-by-case basis. Contact us at info@maisonmerch.ca with your order details and what you'd like to exchange for, and we'll do our best to accommodate you.

Our Bundles

What's included in the Essential Fan Bundle?

The Essential Fan Bundle includes 14–16 curated fan items — everything you need to show up to match day ready. Think flags, wristbands, scarves, and more, all themed around your team or country. Full contents are listed on the product page.

What's included in the Watch Party Bundle?

The Watch Party Bundle is our premium offering — 18–21 pieces designed to transform any space into a full match-day celebration. Includes everything in the Essential bundle plus party decorations, tableware, and atmosphere items. Perfect for hosting groups.

Can I customize a bundle?

Our current bundles are curated sets, but we're exploring customization options. For large or bulk orders, reach out to us at info@maisonmerch.ca — we may be able to put together something tailored for your event.

Eco-Friendly

Are your products really eco-friendly?

Yes — sustainability is core to who we are. All our products are made with eco-friendly materials and responsible manufacturing practices. We believe fan culture and environmental responsibility can coexist, and we're committed to proving it.

What about your packaging?

We use minimal, recyclable packaging wherever possible. We're continuously working to reduce our environmental footprint throughout the supply chain.
