Orders & Products

How do I place an order?

All Maison Merch bundles are purchased directly on Amazon. Click any "Shop Now" or "Shop This Bundle" button on our site — you'll be taken to our Amazon store where you can select your bundle and complete checkout using your Amazon account. Your order confirmation comes from Amazon.

Can I order from outside Canada and the USA?

Our bundles are currently available for fans in the USA, Canada, Mexico, Brazil, and Argentina via Amazon. Shipping availability and timelines are determined by Amazon at checkout based on your delivery address.

Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?

Orders are placed and processed through Amazon, so any changes or cancellations need to be handled through your Amazon account. Go to Returns & Orders in your Amazon account and select the order you wish to cancel or modify. Act quickly — Amazon processes orders fast and cancellations may not be possible once the item has shipped.

Will I get an order confirmation?

Yes — Amazon sends an order confirmation to the email on your Amazon account immediately after purchase, followed by a shipping notification with tracking details once your bundle is dispatched.

Do you offer bulk or wholesale pricing?

Yes — we love supporting event organizers, schools, clubs, and diaspora associations. Reach out to us at info@maisonmerch.ca with your event details and required quantities and we'll put together a custom quote.

Shipping & Delivery

How long does shipping take?

Shipping timelines are set by Amazon and depend on your location and the shipping method you select at checkout. Amazon Prime members generally receive faster delivery. Estimated delivery dates are shown clearly on the Amazon product page before you confirm your order.

Do you offer free shipping?

Shipping rates and any free shipping thresholds are determined by Amazon at checkout. Amazon Prime members may qualify for free and fast shipping on eligible orders. Check the Amazon product page for current shipping offers in your region.

How do I track my order?

Log into your Amazon account and go to Returns & Orders to see real-time tracking on any Maison Merch order. Amazon will also email you tracking updates automatically as your package moves.

What if my package is lost or damaged in transit?

Contact Amazon Customer Service directly — they are available 24/7 and have full authority to file carrier claims, arrange replacements, and issue refunds. This is the fastest path to resolution for any delivery issue.

Returns & Refunds

What is your return policy?

Returns are handled by Amazon under their standard return policy. Most Maison Merch items are eligible for return within 30 days of delivery. Go to Returns & Orders in your Amazon account, find your order, and select Return or Replace Items to get started — no need to contact us first.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are processed by Amazon once they receive your returned item. Amazon typically issues refunds within 3–5 business days to your original payment method. You can track refund status in your Amazon account under Returns & Orders.

Who pays for return shipping?

Amazon covers return shipping for items that arrive damaged, defective, or incorrect. For change-of-mind returns, shipping costs depend on your Amazon membership — Prime members often receive free returns on eligible items. Amazon will show the applicable return options when you initiate the return through your account.

Can I exchange an item?

Exchanges are handled through Amazon. When you initiate a return in your Amazon account, select Replace Items if you'd like an exchange rather than a refund. For further assistance, contact Amazon Customer Service.
