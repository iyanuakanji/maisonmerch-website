At Maison Merch, we want your fan experience to start the moment you place your order. Here's everything you need to know about how we ship and handle returns.

Shipping Information

We ship all orders from our fulfillment centre in Canada. All orders are processed Monday through Friday, excluding Canadian statutory holidays.

Shipping rates and estimated delivery windows are calculated at checkout based on your delivery address and the shipping method you select.

Shipping Zones & Estimated Delivery

We currently ship to Canada and the United States.

Destination Estimated Delivery Free Shipping Threshold
Canada (all provinces) 5–10 business days Orders over $75 CAD
United States 7–14 business days Orders over $75 CAD

Delivery times are estimates only and are not guaranteed. Delays may occur due to carrier backlogs, customs processing, or circumstances beyond our control.

Processing Times

Most orders are processed and dispatched within 1–3 business days of receiving your order. During peak periods (such as major tournaments or holidays), processing may take up to 5 business days. You will receive a shipping notification email as soon as your order leaves our fulfillment centre.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email containing a tracking number and a link to track your package. You can also log into your account on our website to view order status at any time.

If your tracking information hasn't updated within 5 business days of receiving your shipping confirmation, please contact us at info@maisonmerch.ca.

Lost or Damaged Packages: If your order arrives damaged or is reported lost by the carrier, contact us within 7 days of the expected delivery date. We'll file a claim with the carrier and work quickly to make it right — whether that's a replacement shipment or a full refund.

Returns Policy

We want you to love your Maison Merch products. If for any reason you're not satisfied, we accept returns under the following conditions:

  • Returns must be initiated within 30 days of the delivery date
  • Items must be in original, unused condition with all original packaging and tags intact
  • Items must not show signs of use, washing, or alteration
  • A valid proof of purchase (order number or receipt) is required

How to Start a Return

To initiate a return, follow these steps:

  1. Email us at info@maisonmerch.ca with the subject line "Return Request — [Your Order Number]"
  2. Include a brief reason for the return and photos if the item is damaged or defective
  3. Our team will respond within 2 business days with a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number and return instructions
  4. Ship the item back using a trackable shipping method
  5. Once received and inspected, your refund will be processed within 5–7 business days

Who pays for return shipping? If the item is defective or we made an error, we'll cover the return shipping cost. For change-of-mind returns, the customer is responsible for return shipping fees.

Non-Returnable Items

The following items are not eligible for return:

  • Items returned after the 30-day return window
  • Items that show signs of use, washing, or damage caused by the customer
  • Items returned without original packaging or tags
  • Sale items or items purchased with a promotional discount greater than 50% off (unless defective)
  • Digital gift cards
